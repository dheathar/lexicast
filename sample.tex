\section{Introduction}
Curiosity is the engine of discovery. It begins with a simple question:
\emph{why?} From there, the mind follows a thread that may lead anywhere.

\section{An Example}
Consider Einstein's famous relation $E = mc^2$, which connects mass and energy.
A small amount of mass holds an enormous amount of energy.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Observe carefully.
  \item Ask better questions.
  \item Test your ideas.
\end{itemize}
